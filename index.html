Brand pillars

Six promises we design around

  1. 01

    Verification first

    Layered trust — phone, selfie, family, education, employment — shown as a clear trust level.

  2. 02

    Family-centered

    Candidate and guardian accounts, with configurable family-first or candidate-first flows.

  3. 03

    Privacy & control

    Blurred photos, watermarked cards, expiring links. Your profile never travels without you.

  4. 04

    Values-based matching

    Deen, akhlaq, and family compatibility — over appearance, wealth, or status.

  5. 05

    Guided journey

    Optional mediators, counselling, and scholar-reviewed guidance — not just a directory.

  6. 06

    Quality over quantity

    Fewer profiles, better matches, higher trust. No endless scroll, no boosts.
