What Ahlan Rishta is

Ahlan Rishta is a private matrimonial platform built for Indian Muslim families living in Saudi Arabia. Nobody browses a public directory. Every profile is verified before it is seen, you receive a short ranked list of genuine matches rather than a search box, and first contact happens through a staged introduction your family can be part of from the very beginning.

How it works

  1. 01

    One profile, yours or your child's

    You create it, or a parent or wali creates it on your behalf. It covers deen and practice, family, education, profession, your origin in India, your timeline for marriage, and the things you will not compromise on.

  2. 02

    Verification, layer by layer

    Phone, selfie, family, education and employment are checked one at a time — and an imam, employer or community elder can vouch for you directly. Other members see your trust level. They never see your documents.

  3. 03

    Matches, not a search bar

    A short, ranked, explainable list of verified profiles, weighted towards deen and family compatibility. Photos stay blurred and names stay masked until you choose otherwise. Paying never moves anyone up the list.

  4. 04

    Introductions, with family in the room

    Interest opens a staged introduction — family review, questions, family introduction, serious discussion. There is no open chat, no numbers passed off-platform, and a decline is final and quiet.

In plain terms

We are

Who it is for

Come if this sounds like you

This first gathering is for Indian Muslim families living in Saudi Arabia. If one of these three describes your household, the afternoon is worth your time.

  • Marriage seekers

    Working or studying in the Kingdom, serious about nikah within a defined timeline, and tired of searching through WhatsApp forwards, relatives and unverified listings.

  • Parents, guardians and walis

    Searching on behalf of a son or daughter, whether they are here in the Kingdom or back home in India. You can hold and manage the account yourself — a first-class path here, not a workaround.

  • Families searching India and the Gulf at once

    Roots and community in India, life and work in Saudi Arabia, and a search that has to run in both directions — native place, biradari, and relocation all weighed together.

Honestly, not for you if You are looking for casual dating, you are not ready to marry within a defined timeline, or you would rather stay anonymous than be verified. We would rather say that now than take your Friday afternoon.

Before you register

Questions families ask us

Is it free to attend?

Yes. Entry is free and by invitation — you register, we confirm your seat. The complimentary Premium membership every attendee receives is free as well.

Can I bring my parents? Should I?

Yes, and many people will. Candidates and guardians are both welcome, together or separately, and they sit in the same session. If your family will be involved in the decision, bringing them saves you explaining the whole thing twice.

Do I have to sign up to attend?

No. Come, watch the walkthrough, ask your questions and leave. The registration desk on the day is there if you want it, not a condition of entry.

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing is required. If you would like to begin verification at the desk, bringing your iqama — and, if you have them, education or employment documents — will make that quicker. Nothing you bring is kept or copied without your say-so.

Is this only for Indian Muslim families?

This first gathering and our opening phase are focused on Indian Muslim families living in Saudi Arabia. That focus is deliberate — it is how a small, verified pool stays genuinely relevant. We expect to widen across the Gulf after launch.

Where in Riyadh is it, and what time?

We share the exact venue and timing with registered guests a few days beforehand, once we know how many seats are confirmed and can match the room to the number. It is an afternoon gathering.

What happens to the details I give you?

They stay with us. Your email and number are used to confirm your seat and send the venue and timing — nothing else. No marketing lists, no sharing with third parties, and no profile is created for you unless you create one yourself.

Is this a dating app?

No. There is no swiping, no public browsing, and no open chat. Profiles are verified before anyone sees them, photos are private by default, and first contact is a staged introduction your family can be part of from the first step.
