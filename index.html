What we are building

Six things we will not trade away

Ahlan Rishta is not a directory and not a dating app. Nobody browses in public: profiles are verified before they are seen, matches are few and explained, and first contact is a staged introduction your family can be part of from the very beginning.

  1. 01

    Verification first

    Layered trust — phone, selfie, family, education, employment — shown as a clear trust level. Others see the level; they never see your documents.

  2. 02

    Family-centered

    Candidate and guardian accounts, with configurable family-first or candidate-first flows. A parent or wali can hold and manage the profile outright.

  3. 03

    Privacy & control

    Blurred photos, masked names, watermarked cards and expiring links. Your profile never travels without you.

  4. 04

    Values-based matching

    Deen, akhlaq, and family compatibility — over appearance, wealth, or status. Paying never moves anyone up the list.

  5. 05

    Guided journey

    Staged introductions, optional mediators, and scholar-reviewed guidance — not just a directory and a chat box.

  6. 06

    Quality over quantity

    Fewer profiles, better matches, higher trust. No endless scroll, no boosts, no swiping.
